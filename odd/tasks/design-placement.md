# Design placement: move and resize a catalog design anywhere on the garment, saved with the order
Branch `feat/design-catalog` (stacked). Delivery: `ask-on-risk`; QA (`feat/vps-self-hosting`) only when the user says so, never production. Runner: `node --test` (PGlite), plus `npm.cmd run lint` and `DATABASE_URL= npm.cmd run build`.

## Specs
- **S1** User: "La idea sería en que puedan mover la imagen dentro de la remera, no solo dentro del recuadro. Es decir, podrían acomodarlo en la parte en la que quieran." On the store's design page (/disenos/[slug]) the customer can drag the design, change its size and center it, like Personalizados (Custom Lab), anywhere inside the garment zone of the photo, not only inside the print area (dashed box).
- **S2** User: "que puedan elegir un diseño que yo haya subido y elegir si lo quieren al frente o en la espalda, y que puedan ver eso". The customer picks front or back; each side keeps its own placement and starts centred on that side's print area, so switching sides never carries a position that lands on the collar ("cambio de espalda a frente, y el logo de paredes queda mal ubicado ... Queda allá en el cuello").
- **S3** Each template photo has a garment zone (the whole torso, collar to hem). Built-in photos ship with one; Admin › Soportes lets the admin set it for uploaded photos next to the print area, prefilled with a generous default. The print area keeps deciding where the design starts and the cm scale.
- **S4** The size is shown in cm of width (as in Personalizados), capped at 30 cm of width (assumption, from the owner: "la base ... incluye un diseño de 30 cm").
- **S5** User: "dale, guardalo en el pedido y arrancá". Owner: "si puede guardar la imagen nomas tipo jpg de donde el cliente ubico y las medidas prox ya me manejo". The chosen side, width in cm and position travel with the cart line and the order; the admin order view shows the line's mockup image with the measurements, downloadable as JPG.
- **S6** Out of scope now: extra price by size or number of designs ("lo de extra por centímetro, por tramo y demás, no lo haría ahora"); front + back images in Personalizados ("Eso después podríamos revisarlo").
